FAQ

Who is Launch for?

Launch is designed for new agents in their first year, or anyone looking to build momentum with a structured, monthly curriculum.

Do I need to attend all 11 months in order?

We encourage participants to complete the modules in sequence and experience the entire series. That said, every module is designed to stand on its own and can be taken individually.

How much does Launch cost?

Launch is provided to you at no cost as a Member Benefit.
